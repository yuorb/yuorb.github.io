### 💡 Inline Morphological Glosser Component

In addition to the sandbox above, articles across this site natively support the `<IthkuilWord>` inline component. While reading:
* **Hover** over any word (e.g., <IthkuilWord word="wetace" /> or <IthkuilWord word="itrutļalžá" />) to reveal its live morphological breakdown.
* **Click** the word to **instantly copy** its Short Gloss to your clipboard for quick note-taking and community discussions.
